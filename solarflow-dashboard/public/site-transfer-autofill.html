SolarEdge site transfer autofill

Drag this button UP to your bookmarks bar. Do not click it here, it only works on the SolarEdge form. One time, per browser.

No bookmarks bar visible? Press Cmd+Shift+B to show it.

SE Transfer Fill

How to use it

  1. In the service order, open the Site Transfer card and click Submit transfer at SolarEdge. The SolarEdge form opens carrying that order's Site ID and inverter serial.
  2. Click the SE Transfer Fill bookmark. It fills the Customer Number and everything else it can see.
  3. The form expands once the Customer Number validates. Click the bookmark again to fill the fields that just appeared.
  4. Review every field, then submit the form yourself.
  5. On the confirmation screen, click the bookmark once more. It copies the case number to your clipboard.
  6. Back in the service order, paste it into the Site Transfer RMA entry that was created for you.

The bookmarklet never submits the form. It only fills fields, so you always see what is being sent before it goes.
