## 8783ea2: review the SOW before invoicing (Billing)

Live `v1.7.6.0+8783ea2.1787012784175`, builtAt 2026-08-18T00:26:24Z.
Bundle-verified on `assets/Billing-DMcqDPB8.js` (Generate Invoice, Billable
work, Notify Daniel to Invoice, Mark Invoiced, Not yet all present).

### Ask
"Show the SOW report in billing when it is time to create the invoice. He needs
to know what the actual work done was and what he actually needs to invoice.
When clicking generate invoice do not close the card, show the SOW."

### Before
The Ready to Invoice card fired `handleRequestInvoice` straight from the board,
so the order left the column before anyone saw what the contractor did.

### Now
Card action is **Generate Invoice** and it opens the SOW Completion Report over
the board. Nothing advances on click. The card stays in Ready to Invoice until
Daniel acts from inside the report, where the work and the decision share a
screen.

### Reuse, not a rebuild
`SowDistributionModal` already took a plain `Job`, and every field it reads
(`serviceReport`, `nextSteps`, `completionNotes`, `woPhotos`) lives on the type,
so Billing renders it directly. Added ONE optional prop, `actions`, a decision
bar pinned under the report: Not yet / Notify Daniel to Invoice / Mark Invoiced.

It reuses the existing `.sow-toolbar` class, so `PRINT_STYLE`'s
`display:none !important` already keeps the buttons out of the printed PDF. No
new print rule needed.

### GOTCHA that changed the design: SHOW_MONEY
First build put "To invoice {formatMoney(job.totalAmount)}" in the bar. It
rendered literally as **"-"**, and "3 hrs labor -" underneath.

`lib/money.ts` sets `SHOW_MONEY = false` on purpose: commercial figures (quotes,
revenue, client rates, contractor pay) are hidden platform-wide because
financials are handled in Xero and Daniel owns them there. `formatMoney` always
returns `MONEY_HIDDEN`.

The same module documents the carve-out: `formatCost` IS allowed for costs the
user typed in the field (parts, expenses, additional labor), because "the office
needs it to price the quote".

So the bar shows no client total at all. It shows labor hours, service type,
field-entered parts via `formatCost`, and a PowerCare bills-to-SolarEdge flag.

**RULE: before putting a dollar figure in the UI, check which formatter applies.
`formatMoney` is a policy gate, not a formatter, and under SHOW_MONEY=false it
silently turns your feature into a dash.** This is the second time it has bitten
(cf. the "Saved to Date" blank).

### Verification
All 10 Ready to Invoice cards carry the button; opening one left the column at
10; the bar read "3 hrs labor / Optimizer / Microinverter Change" with no
dashes; Not yet closed without moving anything. Build green, 505/505 tests.

### Follow-ups

  `woStatus: 'completed'`) still advances straight to invoiced without showing
  the SOW. Same treatment there was left out of scope; say the word.
