
## a9895ed: the tech sees which visit they are on, and can review the earlier ones

**Ask:** techs need every visit visible on their side, a way to review previous visits, and to add the current visit's photos and report. Service orders often take several trips.

**Already working (verified, not rebuilt):** `toContractorJobView` carries `visits` to the contractor copy and scopes `photos`, `parts`, `additionalItems` and `visitLabor` to the active visit. `JobDetail` stamps `activeVisitId` on every photo (`visitPhotoOwners`), on the report and on labor rows. So the current visit's pictures and report were already landing on the right visit.

**The real gap was visibility:** nothing on the tech screen said which visit they were standing in, and the earlier trips sat at the bottom of the Service Order card under scope, location, photos and notes.

**Shipped:** `components/contractor/VisitBanner.tsx`, above the Service Order card in `JobDetail`. Shows "Visit N of M", the service type and reason the office approved for THIS visit, a line saying photos and the report go on this visit, and a collapsed "Review earlier visits (n)" holding the existing `VisitHistory` (work done, left to do, labor, parts, photos). Removed the duplicate `VisitHistory` from `ServiceOrderCard`; history thumbnails went from 6 to 4 columns at phone width. Renders nothing on a single-visit order.

**Files:** `VisitBanner.tsx` (new), `contractor/JobDetail.tsx`, `contractor/ServiceOrderCard.tsx`, `VisitHistory.tsx`, `__tests__/visitBanner.test.tsx` (new). 1157 tests, build OK.

**Deploy gotchas hit:**
- Local `main` was behind `origin/main` with another session's uncommitted edits in the tree. Rebased by cherry-picking into a throwaway worktree and `git reset --mixed origin/main` after, never stash.
- Vercel's Git auto-deploy never picked the commit up (last prod build stayed 32 min old). Manual deploy also had to come from a clean worktree, since `vercel --prod` ships the working tree and ours held someone else's work in progress: `git worktree add --detach /tmp/so-deploy origin/main`, `cp -R .vercel`, then `vercel --prod` from that root.
- **A CLI deploy reports `version.json` `sha: "local"`,** because the git sha comes from Vercel's git env vars that only a Git-triggered build has. Verify by `builtAt` plus a string from the change in the shipped chunks: "Review earlier visits" is in `assets/index-BmaZOr-3.js`. `/api/users` returns 401 JSON, so the functions built.

**Pending:** `/snap-ui` catalog not refreshed for this one (contractor job screen is not in the manifest, and the capture logs in as staff).
