## Workable lead cards on the LL board: open, log calls/emails, convert to client (fc7047a, v1.7.6.0)

DEPLOYED `fc7047a`, live confirmed on `solarflow-dashboard-sooty.vercel.app` (version.json sha=fc7047a, builtAt 2026-08-13T15:20Z; also grepped the Jobs bundle `Jobs-DMaOrMia.js` for `Move to Client` / `leadInfo` / `Log Call`, all present, since version.json sha is often "local").

### Problem
The "Leads Services SolarEdge" cards are **customerless jobs**. Clicking one did nothing: the card opens `jobDetail`, whose render null-guards on `selectedCustomer` (`if (!selectedJob || !selectedCustomer) return null`), and a lead has no customer. So new webhook leads (and the 5 migrated `job-lead-*` leads) could not be opened or worked at all.

### What shipped
Clicking a **customerless** card now opens a dedicated **LeadPanel** (a linked card still opens the Service Order panel, unchanged):
- Editable contact fields (name/phone/email/address/city/state/zip), stored on the job as **`Job.leadInfo`** (new optional field). Seeded by `seedLeadInfo`: name from `clientName`, phone/email mined from the Trello notes, so nothing is retyped.
- **Log Call / Log Email / Log Note** actions append to the job's `activityHistory` (with the user + timestamp); a click-to-call button fires RingCentral `rcCall(phone)`.
- **Move to Client**: `leadToCustomer(job)` builds a Customer from the lead (carrying the logged call/email activity), `onCreateCustomer` creates it and returns the id, the job is linked (`customerId`) and advanced from `leads` to `needs_first_quote`, and `leadInfo` is cleared.

Model (user's choice): a lead stays a lead, **no customer record until conversion**, so unqualified leads never pollute the customer DB. A converted card carries no `woNumber`, so it stays LL-only (`isPipelineOnly`) until it becomes a real service order.

### Files
- `types/index.ts`: `Job.leadInfo` + `LeadInfo` interface.
- `lib/leadConvert.ts` (new, pure + tested): `seedLeadInfo`, `leadDisplayName`, `leadToCustomer`.
- `components/LeadPanel.tsx` (new): the lead card UI.
- `components/Jobs.tsx`: `handleCardClick` routes customerless cards to `LeadPanel`; `handleConvertLead`; new `onCreateCustomer` prop; renders the panel.
- `App.tsx`: `handleCreateCustomer` now returns the new customer id; passed to both `<Jobs>` renders.
