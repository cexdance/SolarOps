### What changed and why

Daniel asked to automate submitting the SolarEdge site transfer at
`https://www.solaredge.com/site-transfer`. A server-side submission is not
possible, and the reason matters more than the feature:

1. **The page is behind a Cloudflare bot check.** The in-app automation browser
   sits on "Just a moment..." forever and never gets the form. The user's real
   Chrome loads it in about 5 seconds. Getting a headless client through would
   mean defeating bot detection, which is out of bounds.
2. **The form is a Drupal wizard with a per-session token.** Only Customer
   Number renders on load; the other 33 fields exist in the DOM but are hidden
   until it validates, and the POST carries `form_build_id` / `form_id`. A blind
   POST fails even setting Cloudflare aside.

So the fill happens inside the operator's own browser, via a bookmarklet.

### Patterns worth reusing

- **RULE: when a target site is behind bot detection, that is the design
  constraint, not an obstacle to route around.** The whole shape of this feature
  (bookmarklet in the user's browser, never a server POST) falls out of that one
  fact. Establishing it took two minutes and saved building the wrong thing.
- **RULE: an idempotent re-runnable action beats detecting the step.** The form
  reveals fields progressively. Rather than model the wizard, the bookmarklet
  fills everything currently visible and you click it again. Fewer moving parts
  and it cannot get out of sync with a page we do not control.
- **RULE: the id belongs to the SLOT, not the value** (same family as 09-01
  a268edc). The RMA entry id is `rma-sitetransfer-<jobId>`, so clicking Submit
  twice reuses one entry instead of stacking duplicates.
- **The bookmarklet never submits.** Fill-only means the payload is always
  reviewed by a human before it goes. Deliberate, not a limitation.
- **GOTCHA: `cd` persists between Bash calls in a session.** `mkdir -p public`
  landed in `solarflow-dashboard/src/public/` because an earlier `cd public` was
  still in effect. Use absolute paths for mkdir.
- **GOTCHA: vitest `include` is `src/__tests__/**/*.test.{ts,tsx}`**, not the
  `*.test.ts`-anywhere the memory index claimed. A test outside that directory
  exits "No test files found", which reads like a pass.
- **GOTCHA: `new URL(path, import.meta.url)` throws "The URL must be of scheme
  file" under vitest.** Use `resolve(process.cwd(), ...)` to read a fixture.
- **TRAP: I read a stale rtk tee log and reported a passing test as failing.**
  Piping vitest to `/dev/null` left the previous run's JSON as the newest log.
  Re-run and read the run's own output.
