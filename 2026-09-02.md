### What was wrong
The Billing list view was not missing filters, it was filtering by the wrong vocabulary. The list only had the coarse `all / unbilled / invoiced / paid` dropdown, which cannot express "Ready to Invoice" or "Costs Covered", the two stages the board actually runs on. Result: one undifferentiated 151-row scroll sitting next to a kanban that sliced the same records seven ways with `getBillingColumn`.

### What changed
- `BILLING_COLUMNS` hoisted to module scope in `Billing.tsx`. The kanban columns and the list stage filter now read from one array, so a renamed stage is renamed in both places.
- Stage chips with live counts above the list, persisted to `solarops_billing_list_stage`.
- The coarse status dropdown is hidden in list mode. Two status controls on one toolbar is how a user filters themselves into an empty screen with no idea which control did it.
- `matchesSearch` extracted from the inline filter chain so list and kanban share the search predicate (the empty-query guard from 08-24 c0bfdee is preserved verbatim).
- Empty state names the stage and offers "Show all stages".
- Active chip is a solid stage-colored fill, not a pastel tint.

### Patterns worth reusing
- **RULE: when two views of the same records disagree on their filter vocabulary, the one with fewer categories is usually the broken one.** The kanban had 7 stages, the list had 4 statuses, and the 4 were not a coarsening of the 7, they were a different axis. Adding filters to the list would have been the wrong fix; adopting the kanban's axis was the right one.
- **RULE: counts that do not sum are a trust bug on a money screen.** First build shipped All (151) against stages summing to 127. Archived orders counted in All but belonged to no stage. 24 orders silently unaccounted for. Archived is now excluded from All too. **Always add up the counts you put on screen before shipping them.**
- **GOTCHA: an active state tinted in the stage's own pastel can be weaker than another chip's hover state.** The slate "Pending Completion" active chip was indistinguishable from a hovered inactive chip. Solid fill for selection, tint for nothing.
- Verification caught both of the above, not the diff. Screenshots of live data, not the code, are what showed the numbers not adding up.
