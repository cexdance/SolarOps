## 2fb29f6: mobile menu showed the technician set, no Sign Out

**Report:** on the phone the sidebar only had Ops Center, Dashboard, Customers, Service Orders, Messages, plus My Jobs, and no user/Sign Out section.

**Cause:** `currentUser` was empty. Layout falls back to `role ?? 'technician'` and only renders the user section when `currentUser` exists. The startup pull (`adopt` in App.tsx) and the poll in `useSyncEngine` both spread the stored snapshot over state, including `currentUser` (undefined in the snapshot). On slow LTE the pull lands after session restore and erased the user. Desktop wins the race, so it looked mobile-only.

**Fix:** both paths keep `prev.currentUser ?? snapshot.currentUser`. Also Dispatch Map (parent `jobs`) was gated on `customersExpanded`; now always shown under Service Orders.

**Files:** `src/App.tsx`, `src/hooks/useSyncEngine.ts`, `src/components/Layout.tsx`.

**Verified:** build + 1040 tests pass; committed tree type-checks clean in a separate worktree (another session's uncommitted `ServiceOrderPanel.tsx` / `contractorStore.ts` edits were left out). Live `version.json` sha 2fb29f6, `/api/users` 401.

**Pattern:** a sync adopt that spreads a stored snapshot must not carry session state (`currentUser`) across. Session state belongs to auth, not storage.

**Follow-up:** owner to confirm on the phone after a full close/reopen.
