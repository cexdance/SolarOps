## 94d9a67: cancel a follow-up visit added by mistake

**Ask:** owner opened a second visit on SO-2609-61705 that was not a second visit, and there was no way back.

**Feature:** `cancelVisit(job, actor, now)` in `api/_serviceVisits.ts`, the exact inverse of `requestVisit`: pops the last archived visit back onto the job (its `billing` keys, `date`, `serviceType`, `workDone`, `serviceStatus`, `nextSteps`, `startedAt`, `finishedAt` -> `completedAt`), restores `currentVisit` to that visit's own `plan` (deleted when it had none), drops the cancelled plan's `visitLabor` / `contractorParts`, and stamps `cancelledVisitAt` / `cancelledVisitBy`.

- Stage is derived from the restored billing (`clientPaidAt` -> paid, `invoicedAt` -> invoiced, else completed). A visit archive carries its billing, not its column, so an exact stage restore is not possible; derivation is the honest answer.
- Guard: refuses once the new visit has work of its own (`startedAt`, `completedAt`, report, notes, labor/parts tagged to it, or photos owned by it). Cancel is for a mistake, not for undoing a real visit.
- Endpoint: `action: 'cancel'` on `/api/service-visits`, admin/coo only (existing gate), `expectedVisitId` checked like approve/include.
- UI: "Added by mistake, cancel this visit" in `VisitWorkspace`, admin-only, shown only when a previous visit exists, behind a `confirm`.

## 8a153d5: a cancelled visit cannot be resurrected by a stale copy

The gap left by 94d9a67: `cancelVisit` only cleared the admin row, while every merge unions visits by id, so a contractor phone or a second tab still holding the follow-up fed it straight back.

**Tombstones, `id@cutoff`, in `job.cancelledVisitIds`.** `cancelVisit` writes BOTH halves of the split: the plan it opened AND the visit it archived, each stamped with the cancel time.

**Why the cutoff.** Visit ids are positional (`job:visit:2`), so a genuine later follow-up takes the id just cancelled. A bare id tombstone killed it, caught by the "genuine new follow-up" test before ship. `isCancelledVisit` treats a record as dead only when its own stamp (`finishedAt`/`updatedAt` for visits, `decidedAt ?? requestedAt` for plans, `updatedAt` for labor) is STRICTLY before the cutoff. Everything the cancel removed was created earlier; anything stamped at or after it is real work.

**Every merge honours it:** `mergeVisitRecords` (visits union), `mergeJobFields` (the higher-visit-number branch AND the newest-plan branch, plus the visit-labor merge and a final plan check), `toContractorJobView` (the contractor copy also receives `cancelledVisitIds`), the contractor reconcile and the panel save in `App.tsx`. Tombstones union on merge, so neither side can drop them.

**GOTCHA fixed:** `mergeJobFields`' "higher visit number wins" copied `currentVisit` plus the whole billing cycle (status, woStatus, quote, invoice keys) from whichever side had the bigger number. A stale copy therefore beat the repaired record outright, not just on the visit list.
